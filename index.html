What health insurance coverage is available to Unit 1 members of CUPE 3902?

As well as the UTGSU health plan, members of Unit 1 (Teaching Assistants, Course Instructors, Exam Invigilators, Chief Presiding Officers, Tutors, and Markers) are entitled to access health benefits under the terms of the 2024-2026 Collective Agreement. All health benefits are administered by GreenShield.

The 2024-2026 Collective Agreement expires on December 31, 2026. For updates on bargaining for the next agreement, see the 2026-27 Coordinated Bargaining Campaign page.

The terms of the 2024-2026 Collective Agreement state that in order to access health benefits, Unit 1 members must have worked and received payment for a minimum of 15 hours of Unit 1 work during the current academic year (September 1 – August 31) prior to July 31. While health insurance claims can only be made once you have worked and been paid for 15 hours of Unit 1 work, your coverage period is for the full academic year (September 1 – August 31). Ensure that you keep receipts in order to make retroactive claims.

Two components of the CUPE 3902 Unit 1 health plan exist: Plan A (The "Top-Up" Plan) and Plan B (The "HCSA" Plan). All graduate students at UofT are automatically enrolled in the UTGSU health plan. This base plan provides additional access to Plan A as a "top up" for Unit 1 members. Unit 1 members who have actively opted out of the UTGSU health plan, whether the health coverage, the dental coverage, or both, may only receive Plan B.

Unit 1 members who are not graduate students access Plan A through their own base plan (UTSU, SCSU, APUS, or the PDF plan for postdoctoral fellows), and must be enrolled in both its health and dental coverage.

Important coverage dates and deadlines

All Plans:

  • The coverage period for the UTGSU health plan, Plan A, and Plan B is September 1 – August 31
  • From September 1 – October 31 there is a blackout period, where UofT confirms the eligibility of members and sends the list to GreenShield
  • During the blackout period, you are covered but cannot make claims, and you may have difficulty registering your account on GreenShield's website if you are a new graduate student
  • Once the blackout period has ended, you can start submitting retroactive health insurance claims to GreenShield dating back to September 1 – keep your receipts!
  • The last day to use the GreenShield website for making claims online prior to the start of the blackout period for a new academic year is August 30 (not August 31)
  • After August 30, once the blackout period for a new academic year begins, claims for the previous year can be sent to GreenShield manually via downloadable paper forms (see Making manual claims with GreenShield)
  • Claims for the previous year (September 1 – August 31) must be received by (as opposed to sent to) GreenShield by October 30 (not October 31)

CUPE 3902 Unit 1 Plan A and Plan B:

  • If you complete and receive payment for a minimum of 15 hours of Unit 1 work prior to July 31, you will have health insurance from Plan A or Plan B for the entirety of the coverage period (September 1 – August 31)
  • Members who have worked 15 hours or more can expect to be enrolled approximately 4 weeks following their first pay cycle. Hours are calculated on an average between months, so check your monthly pay stub.
  • After you have received pay for 15 hours of work, you can start submitting retroactive health insurance claims to GreenShield dating back to September 1 – keep your receipts!

How do I access my health insurance?

You have different account ID numbers with GreenShield for your UTGSU health plan and your CUPE 3902 Unit 1 health plan (either Plan A or Plan B). They are respectively based on your student number and your personnel/employee number.

UTGSU health plan UTG[student number]-00

Your student number should be on your T-Card. You can also find it on the finance page of ACORN at the top of your tuition invoice.

For example, if your student number is 1234432111, your GreenShield ID number for your UTGSU health plan is UTG1234432111-00

CUPE 3902 Unit 1 Plan A or Plan B UTA[personnel number]-00

Your employee/personnel number can be found on paystubs, through contacting your Departmental Finance Officer, and can also be found on the top left corner of your pay slips on the Employee Self-Service website (login with UTORid).

For example, if your personnel/employee number is 1001234, your GreenShield ID number for your CUPE 3902 Unit 1 health plan (either Plan A or Plan B) is UTA1001234-00

These numbers are used by health providers who can directly bill GreenShield for services (for example: your pharmacy). They are also required to register your accounts on GreenShield's website, where you can submit health insurance claims from services which are not directly billable to GreenShield (for example: retroactive claims made on your CUPE 3902 plan, for health services which you paid for out-of-pocket prior to being paid for 15 hours of Unit 1 work).

If you have not opted out of your UTGSU health plan, and therefore have both the UTGSU health plan and the CUPE 3902 Unit 1 Plan A: claims for overlapping services should first be processed through the UTGSU plan, as Plan A is a "top up" for this plan.

Health providers can keep both account ID numbers on file, and you can toggle between both plans on GreenShield's website once you register your accounts online.

How do I register my account(s) online with GreenShield?

In order to make claims online, set up direct deposit, view your benefits, and access other relevant information, you can register your account number(s) on the GreenShield website: https://www.greenshield.ca/en-ca.

Please note that you may have difficulty registering a new account during the blackout period (September 1 – October 31). If you still have difficulty registering your account(s) following the completion of the blackout period, please get in touch with GreenShield at 1-888-525-7587. CUPE 3902 does not have control over website issues related to GreenShield.

If you have not opted out of the UTGSU health plan, this is how to register the UTGSU health plan and CUPE 3902 Unit 1 Plan A on GreenShield's website:
  1. Go to https://www.greenshield.ca/en-ca. Click the 'Sign In' button, and select 'GreenShield+ member sign in' from the drop-down menu.
  2. At the sign in page, select the 'Don't have an account? Sign up' option beneath the login options.
  3. Select the option to access GreenShield+ through an employer or organization, and continue.
  4. Select the 'I have a Member ID' option, and continue.
  5. Enter the email address you wish to link to your health insurance plans.
  6. Enter the verification code sent by GreenShield to your email. Make sure you check Spam and Junk inboxes.
  7. Complete the Member Details section, then continue. Use your UTGSU health plan Member ID, which is formatted as follows: UTG[student number]-00. See How do I access my health insurance? for an example of how this number is formatted, and how to find your student number.
  8. Complete your Profile under the Account Settings, available under the dropdown menu attached to your name on the top right of your account homepage.
  9. Once you have registered your UTGSU health plan, you can now register your CUPE 3902 Unit 1 Plan A. Use the dropdown menu on the top right of the website to Logout.
  10. Once returned to the GreenShield website home page, follow the first steps you took to sign up for your UTGSU health plan:
    • Click the 'Sign In' button, and select 'GreenShield+ member sign in' from the drop-down menu.
    • At the sign in page, select the 'Don't have an account? Sign up' option beneath the login options.
    • Select the option to access GreenShield+ through an employer or organization, and continue.
    • Select the 'I have a Member ID' option, and continue.
  11. Once you are prompted to enter your email address, enter the same email address you used to register your UTGSU health plan.
  12. GreenShield will indicate that you have an existing account affiliated with this email address. Select the 'Register Another User' option to add your second plan to the same account.
  13. Complete the Member Details section, then continue. Use your CUPE 3902 Unit 1 plan Member ID which is formatted as follows: UTA[personnel number]-00. See How do I access my health insurance? for an example of how this number is formatted, and how to find your personnel/employee number.
  14. You should now have access to both accounts, which you can switch between. 'University of Toronto Graduate Students' Union' refers to your UTGSU health plan, and 'University of Toronto' refers to your CUPE 3902 Unit 1 Plan A.
If you have opted out of the UTGSU health plan, this is how to register the CUPE 3902 Unit 1 Plan B on GreenShield's website:
  1. Go to https://www.greenshield.ca/en-ca. Click the 'Sign In' button, and select 'GreenShield+ member sign in' from the drop-down menu.
  2. At the sign in page, select the 'Don't have an account? Sign up' option beneath the login options.
  3. Select the option to access GreenShield+ through an employer or organization, and continue.
  4. Select the 'I have a Member ID' option, and continue.
  5. Enter the email address you wish to link to your health insurance plan.
  6. Enter the verification code sent by GreenShield to your email. Make sure you check Spam and Junk inboxes.
  7. Complete the Member Details section, then continue. Use your CUPE 3902 Unit 1 health plan Member ID which is formatted as follows: UTA[personnel number]-00. See How do I access my health insurance? for an example of how this number is formatted, and how to find your personnel number.
  8. Complete your Profile under the Account Settings, available under the dropdown menu attached to your name on the top right of your account homepage.

Making manual claims with GreenShield

The last day to use the GreenShield website for making claims online prior to the start of the blackout period for a new academic year is August 30 (not August 31).

During the blackout period (September 1 – October 31), claims from the previous coverage year can be made manually by printing out paper forms from GreenShield's website and mailing them to the address provided on the forms. These paper claims forms must be received by (as opposed to sent to) GreenShield by October 30 (not October 31).

The form can be downloaded from GreenShield for printing here: General Claim/Dental Claim Form (PDF)

If it is your preference, you may also make manual claims with GreenShield generally. It is recommended that you call GreenShield for assistance at 1-888-711-1119 (CUPE 3902 Unit 1 plan) or 1-888-525-7587 (UTGSU health plan).
